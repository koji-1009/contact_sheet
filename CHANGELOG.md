## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- The command is `sheet`; the package, `contact_sheet`.
- `sheet agent` and `sheet manual`: the playbook and the reference for the AI agent that writes an app's sheet (`contact_sheet/sheet.yaml`); `sheet -h` points an agent to them.
- The sheet: pages and their states (a `@Preview` each, or one `Preview` of a `MultiPreview`; `data` for those what the page loads decides), the conditions the app keeps across its pages (`conditions`: signed in, what is in the cart; the states each value is the look of), transitions (from one page, several or every page, to a page or, a pop, back to whichever opened it, what the user does (the back written takes the back's place), in which states, while which values of the conditions, the values it gives, the state it arrives in, by the state it leaves or, unsaid, the one of the same name or of the conditions, how the code moves), tab bars whose tabs keep their own history (`tabs`: the pages each tab shows; switched between by the bar, as it does), deep links (the state, the conditions and the tab they open in), and, for reference, dependencies, fakes, memos and links. A mistake in it is told with where it is, a field misspelt or out of place too.
- Variants: every state shot as iOS or Android, and as the `Preview` of a name a `MultiPreview` gives (a tablet, a desktop), switched between in the HTML.
- `sheet init`: an empty sheet; with `--package`, the sheet package's skeleton, for the previews the app does not have.
- `sheet export`: shoots the `@Preview`s the sheet names with shutter, drawn as iOS (the app's in the app, the sheet package's in it), and writes the map into `contact_sheet/map/`: `index.html`, `map.svg`, `map-dev.svg`, `sheet.json` (`schemaVersion` 1; each transition and deep link with a `key` that stays from one export to the next, and where each transition arrives from each state) and `images/`. `--only` shoots some pages alone, `--locale` sets the map's language, `--strict` fails while a state has no image.
- `index.html`: the map (each section an island, laid in rows; bundled arrows from other sections), the tree (the pages top to bottom, each under the page the app's start first reaches it from; `tree` in `sheet.json`), the list of the pages, the table of the transitions, the route between two pages (the fewest steps as the navigator goes, its back included, following the conditions and the tabs' own histories, from a state, the conditions' values and the history the app's start reaches it with), a page's transitions as sentences, an arrow tapped as a sentence, its states on the map, search, an address for each view, page, state, route and page's details, how much the map shows (the pages alone, with the arrows, or with what they say; the arrows back shown or hidden), the parts of an arrow's label drawn apart (what is done, the states and values it needs, a redirect), which app and how current, the developers' information behind "For developers", light and dark, a phone's width, and print.
- `sheet lint`: what the sheet itself shows may be missing (a page no arrow leads to, a state nothing leaves from or arrives in, a condition's value nothing gives, a page with no state for some of the conditions' values, a transition's condition no way through the app meets) or written twice; `export` tells it too.
- `sheet basis` and `sheet changed` (`--by-page`): what the sheet was written against, and the app's files changed since; the map marks the pages they touch and counts the changes the sheet names for no page.
- `sheet prune`: deletes the runs of shutter's Contact Sheet left.
- The command speaks English; the map is in English or Japanese, the machine's language or `--locale`'s.
